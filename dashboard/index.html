Turn off the car

Turn off the car?

The car stops (motors to neutral), the car software ends, the controller turns off and the Pi shuts down. Everyone connected is disconnected. To start it again, unplug the Pi's power and plug it back in.

View-only link

Anyone with this link sees the video and the data. They can't drive, stop the car, change settings or play sounds.

Calibrate the camera lens

  1. Open the checkerboard and print it, or show it full screen on another screen.
  2. Tap Start hands-free, then stand in front of the car holding the board flat and fully in view.
  3. Move it slowly around the picture: centre, corners, edges, tilted, closer and further. The car captures by itself each time it sees the board somewhere new (this device beeps), and finishes after 12.
